In today’s hyper-connected world, the promise of “data-driven” marketing is constantly challenged by a single, frustrating reality: data often conflicts. As the number of measurement systems—from internal tools to third-party providers—multiplies, so does the confusion. Marketers and analysts are left grappling with conflicting metrics, making confident, strategic decision-making feel nearly impossible.
